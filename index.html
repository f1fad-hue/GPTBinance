Sources & method

Math and limits

The optimizer searches every 5%-increment allocation, keeps each required holding at 5% or more, and limits BMNR to 10%. Objective = forecast net CAGR minus 35% of forecast uncertainty. Each candidate must keep its actual monthly-rebalanced common-period portfolio MDD and every named simultaneous stress loss within the applicable 20% / 25% / 30% cap. It must also limit the modeled probability of exceeding that cap over 10 years to 10%.

The combined historical path begins when all four holdings have authoritative live observations, so it is much shorter than ten years because BMNR has limited Nasdaq history. Correlations, stress shocks, forecast returns, uncertainty and simulations are transparent assumptions—not authoritative facts or guarantees. The displayed rate blends 40% broad and 60% allocation-correlated macro sentiment.
