Methodology

Expected net return = scenario gross CAGR − stated annual fund fee (zero for the individual stock). Composite drawdown = 60% historical proxy + 40% forward median simulation drawdown. The displayed cap is then applied according to the portfolio rate: 3.00–3.99 → 20%; 4.00–4.99 → 25%; 5.00 → 30%.

The optimizer maximizes net CAGR subject to long-only weights, required holdings, 10%–45% single-asset bounds, the score-derived drawdown cap, and the listed macro tilts. It uses transparent model assumptions because a reliable 10-year live return series does not exist for every required instrument.
